THE DUCKSHIP
콩이를 위한 배

01
Tomorrow was the pond parade. Ivy and Pip had only wreckage: snapped boards, a torn sail, and half a boat. Bean stayed beside them.

02
“We can build a magnificent ship!” Ivy said. “Imagine towers! Flags! A castle that floats!” Pip wasn’t so sure.

03
“These arrows confuse me,” Pip said. “Does the kitchen go inside the tower, or does the tower go inside the kitchen?” Ivy’s ears slowly drooped.

04
Ivy covered the castle with a blank sheet. “Let’s approach the problem one piece at a time. First, a boat that floats.” Pip sorted the boards.

05
Bean’s little bell reminded Ivy of their first trip: two friends, one duck, and a picnic. “There’s room for everyone,” she said.

06
By dusk, only the red sail was left to fit. Bean kept a watchful eye on it. The corner began to lift.

07
Then the whole sail lifted. Ivy grabbed empty air. Bean jumped.

08
SPLASH! Ivy ran to the edge. “Bean?” Only circles answered.

09
A white head popped up. A red corner hung from Bean’s beak. Pip’s knees went wobbly with relief. Bean looked extremely pleased with himself.

10
In the morning, they fastened the sail. At last, the boat was complete. Nothing on their little plan was missing.

11
Mayor Moss checked the seats, the hull, and the sail. Then she stamped the form. “I approve this boat for the parade.”

12
The mayor offered Ivy a ribbon. Ivy gently pinned it to Bean’s scarf. “For your devotion,” she said. Bean leaned into her hand.

13
They sailed into the parade. The castle plan was a captain’s hat now. Their boat was small. Bean’s hat was not.
